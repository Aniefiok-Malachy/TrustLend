How a Round Moves

  1. 01
    Lender funds the pool

    Sets the ordered borrower queue and sends BNB. Position 1 receives it immediately.

  2. 02
    Borrower uses the funds

    For education, business, or healthcare — whatever they described off-chain.

  3. 03
    Borrower repays

    They send funds back to the contract, then call repay to forward the balance.

  4. 04
    Next borrower is funded

    The queue advances automatically, repeating until the last borrower repays.

  5. 05
    Balance returns to lender

    Once the queue is exhausted, repayments flow back to whoever started the round.
